# A Website for Deco Center — One-Page Proposal

**Prepared for:** Deco Center furniture showroom, 2F Deco Center Complex, Nyarutarama
**Demo:** _<demo link>_ — open it on your phone

## The problem

- **Customers can't find you online.** Someone in Kigali searching "office furniture Kigali" or "sofa Nyarutarama" today finds competitors, not Deco Center.
- **Your products are invisible before the visit.** People want to see options and styles before they drive to a showroom. Today they can only see what's in front of them.
- **Business clients need something to share.** An office manager furnishing 20 desks needs a link they can send to their boss — not a phone number.
- **Enquiries are scattered.** Questions arrive by phone, WhatsApp and email without saying which product they're about.

## The solution

A fast, mobile-first website that turns visitors into WhatsApp conversations with your sales team.

| Feature | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| **Product catalogue** | Chairs, office desks, couches and office sets with photos and short descriptions. |
| **"Chat on WhatsApp" on every product** | One tap opens WhatsApp to your sales team with the product name and reference already typed. Your team knows exactly what the customer wants. |
| **Business clients section** | A clear path for offices, hotels and schools to request bulk quotes. |
| **Showroom page** | Address, map, directions, opening hours and contacts. |
| **Google Business Profile** | Appear on Google Maps and in local search results. |
| **Built for phones** | Most customers will open it on a phone — it loads fast even on mobile data. |

No online payments or accounts needed: you keep selling the way you do today, with more and better-informed enquiries.
