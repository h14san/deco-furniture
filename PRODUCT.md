# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, given equal weight:

- **Homeowners** in the Nyarutarama / Gacuriro / Kagugu area of Kigali. They are mostly higher-income households furnishing a living room, dining room or home office. Most browse on a phone, often on mobile data, before deciding whether the drive to the showroom is worth it.
- **Office and business buyers**, such as office managers, hotels, schools and startups, furnishing anything from 5 desks to a head office. They need a link they can send to whoever approves the spend, and a quick route to a bulk quote.

## Product Purpose

The site is a showroom catalogue that turns visitors into WhatsApp conversations with the sales team. Visitors browse chairs, office desks, couches and office sets. Every product carries a one-tap WhatsApp message with the product name and reference already filled in. Success means more enquiries, and better-informed ones, each saying exactly which product the customer means. A second success is more visits to the physical showroom.

## Positioning

A physical showroom on the 2nd floor of the Deco Center Complex, in one of Kigali's newer malls. Customers can see a piece online, then sit on it before buying. The store covers both home and office, from a single armchair to a complete office fit-out. (Delivery and setup terms are unconfirmed; don't claim them.)

## Operating Context

- Enquiries go to the sales team on WhatsApp and phone. Email (decocenter@homartgroup.com) is a secondary channel.
- The showroom has fixed opening hours. Directions run through Google Maps.
- The store appears linked to Homart Group through the email domain. This is unconfirmed, so the site must not claim it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A static site in `public/`, with no build step, deployed through GitHub Pages. Store details live in `public/js/config.js` and the catalogue in `public/js/products.js`.
- No online payments, accounts or cart. Prices are "on request" through WhatsApp.
- **Undecided, so these must stay easy to change:**
  - the store's real name ("Deco Center" is a placeholder)
  - the sales contacts' names and phone numbers (placeholders: Sales Contact 1/2, +250 7XX XXX XXX)
  - opening hours
  - whether prices will be published
  - who updates the catalogue

## Product Principles

1. Every page leads to a conversation. A WhatsApp action is never more than one tap away, and it always says which product it's about.
2. Show the furniture first. The catalogue is the proof, and claims never replace it.
3. Fast on a phone over mobile data.
4. Home buyers and office buyers each find their path within seconds, and neither is treated as an afterthought.
5. Easy to hand over. Staff can change the name, contacts and products in one place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The content is in English. Kinyarwanda and French may come later, so layouts should allow for longer strings. WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, and respect for reduced motion.
